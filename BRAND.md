## Layout & Grid

### Navigation
- Minimal fixed top bar
- Logo (thetangle.io or wordmark) left, `work · writing · about` links right
- Thin, unobtrusive — stays out of the way of content

### Home / Work Index
- Editorial card grid (2–3 columns), cards start immediately — no hero section
- Each card: preview image + title + short description

### Story / Viz Pages (Scrollytelling)
- **Full-screen viz** — D3 chart fills the entire viewport, sticky
- **Narrative as floating scroll cards** — text appears as overlaid cards anchored to scroll position
- Most immersive option — lets the visualization be the primary experience
- Each story page can define its own background color / visual world
