# thewildplot.io — Story & Viz Ideas

## Format Key
- [S] = Scrollytelling piece (Pudding-style)
- [C] = Chart/visualization (static or interactive)
- [B] = Blog post / written piece
- [D] = Data story (numbers-driven narrative)

---

## Ideas

### [S] Nobody Tells You
Honest data on first-time parenthood — the truths well-meaning parents soften or forget.
7 charts: sleep loss arc, relationship U-curve, 31% career earnings penalty (women),
parental loneliness (66%), paternal PPD peaking at 12 months (not birth), identity shift
(matrescence), and the happiness paradox (lower daily happiness + higher meaning,
simultaneously). Ends on the happiness paradox — not despair. Spring publish for
baby shower season. Full plan: `stories/nobody-tells-you.md`

### [S] 18 Summers
How many fully present summers do you actually have with your child before they leave?
The answer is not 18. BLS Time Use Survey + child development research + personalized
interactive grid. Bittersweet, urgent. Ends in instruction not despair.
Full plan: `stories/18-summers.md`

### [S/Series] Tracking Theo
Personal longitudinal data story using the personal logger. Dada counter + vocabulary
ledger + laugh calendar + morning log. A father counting what most people only try to
remember. Unique dataset — nobody else has this. Series format, building to a long-form
finale. Start logging immediately.
Full plan: `stories/tracking-theo.md`

### [S] The Money Map — Where America's Wealth Is Actually Moving
IRS SOI county-to-county migration data (1991–2023). Core insight: headcount tells one
story, income tells another. Manhattan gained residents but lost $922M in AGI. Income
selectivity ratio (avg AGI of in-movers / out-movers) is the key metric nobody shows.
COVID pivot + persistence angle. Explorer tools exist but no narrative scrollytelling piece.
Full plan: `stories/migration-income.md`

### [S] How Many Times Will You See Your Parents?
seeyourfolks.com gave you a cold number in 2013. This is the piece behind the number.
Real data: BLS visit frequency by life stage, SSA longevity tables, CDC cognitive decline
curves by age. The key insight: remaining visits ≠ remaining *full-presence* visits.
The quality window closes before the lifespan does. Ends in a call to action, not grief.
Holiday season timing. Full plan: `stories/how-many-times.md`

### [S] The Half-Your-Life Moment
There's a calculable date when you've lived as many days after something as before it.
Half your life ago you were 17. Half your time since you left home was age 27.
Chain of half-moments + time compression research (each year feels shorter because it's
a smaller fraction of total lived experience). SSA actuarial tables for forward look.
Reorientation, not despair. Natural companion to 18 Summers and How Many Times.
Full plan: `stories/half-your-life.md`

### [S] Northeast Foliage Forecast 2026
Target publish: September 2026
Choropleth map + calendar strip showing predicted peak foliage dates by county across the Northeast.
Driven by historical climate data + 2026 summer temp/drought anomalies.
Full plan: `stories/foliage-2026.md`
